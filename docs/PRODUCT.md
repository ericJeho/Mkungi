# Mkungi — product brief

> **From Paper to Perfectly Editable.**

*Mkungi* evokes a bundle or collection in Chichewa — fitting for a platform that gathers everything on a page and reconstructs it into an editable file.

**Alternative taglines:** Scan. Convert. Edit. · AI That Rebuilds Documents. · Your Documents, Fully Editable. · The Smart OCR for Business Documents. · Preserve Every Detail. Edit Everything.

## Vision

To become Africa's leading AI-powered document digitisation platform, transforming paper documents into perfectly editable digital files without compromising their original design.

## Mission

To help businesses, governments, NGOs, schools and individuals digitise documents with AI that preserves layouts, tables, branding and formatting while producing professional Word and Excel files.

## What makes Mkungi different

Traditional OCR extracts only text. Mkungi reconstructs the entire document so the output looks almost identical to the original:

| Element | How it is preserved |
|---|---|
| Fonts, colours, spacing | Size, weight, colour, indents, tab stops and spacing inferred per line |
| Tables, borders | Rebuilt as real Word/Excel tables with merged cells and widths |
| Headers & footers, watermarks | Detected as repeating / faint page regions (phase 3) |
| Logos, images | Cropped and placed as pictures |
| Signatures, stamps | Kept **as images** — never re-drawn or OCR'd |
| QR codes, barcodes | Kept as images; decoded value stored as metadata (phase 3) |

## Core products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mkungi OCR** | Convert scanned documents into editable Word or Excel files |
| **Mkungi Templates** | Invoices, quotations, receipts, delivery notes, GRNs, purchase orders, payroll, asset registers, bank reconciliations, financial statements, inventory sheets, timesheets, petty cash vouchers |
| **Mkungi AI** | Identifies the document type and selects the best conversion method |
